**Design Decisions**  
1. **Decompose into three domain-aligned services** – Enables independent development, scaling, and evolution of pricing, hotel, and rule logic.  
2. **Pricing Service orchestrates recalculation** – Ensures consistency between base price changes and derived prices; avoids pushing logic to UI or database.  
3. **Shared database with schema separation** – Balances data consistency and simplicity for MVP; future iterations may split databases if needed.  
4. **Synchronous REST APIs** – Meets <100 ms latency target for query/modification under expected load.
